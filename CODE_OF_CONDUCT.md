# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Lacey Framework welcomes people who use, question, test, criticize, or improve the work. We are
committed to a community where participation is safe, respectful, and available to people of every
background, identity, experience level, and point of view.

Strong disagreement is part of the project. Personal harm is not.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- engage honestly and in good faith;
- criticize claims, evidence, and designs rather than people;
- respect different experiences and viewpoints;
- accept correction and take responsibility for impact;
- respect stated boundaries and confidentiality; and
- help repair harm when possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or unwanted personal attention;
- discriminatory, demeaning, or sexualized language or conduct;
- personal attacks or sustained disruption after a request to stop;
- publishing or using another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, retaliation for a good-faith report, or evasion of moderation; and
- spam, deceptive promotion, or other conduct that undermines safe participation.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. It
also applies when someone is formally representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report possible violations privately to **miked@veldt-ai.com**. Do not place sensitive conduct
reports or personal information in a public issue.

Include links, screenshots, dates, and context when available. Reports will be handled as privately
as practical, consistent with investigating the concern and protecting participants. The project is
maintained with limited capacity and does not promise a specific response time.

If a report concerns the sole maintainer and direct reporting is not safe or appropriate, use the
reporting and blocking tools provided by the platform hosting the interaction.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a private or public warning, limit participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant. The response will reflect the severity,
pattern, and impact of the conduct. Serious threats, doxxing, or sustained harassment may justify
immediate removal.

Enforcement should protect participants, preserve relevant evidence, avoid unnecessary disclosure,
and leave room for repair where repair is possible.
