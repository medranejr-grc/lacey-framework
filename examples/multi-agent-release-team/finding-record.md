# Review Finding Record

Use one record per material finding. Store completed records with the release work so dispositions
remain inspectable.

```text
Finding ID:
Reviewer role:
Artifact and section:
Severity: blocker | material | improvement
Claim or boundary at issue:
Evidence:
Consequence if unchanged:
Recommended repair:

Disposition: accepted | rejected | escalated | deferred
Coordinator rationale:
Files changed:
Second review required: yes | no
Second-review result:
Owner decision or waiver, if required:
```

## Severity and disposition rules

- **Blocker:** publication could expose protected material, materially misstate evidence or
  authorship, violate a license, or cross an authority boundary. Do not publish until repaired or
  explicitly waived by the human owner. A substantial repair requires a second review.
- **Material:** a skeptical reader could be meaningfully misled or unable to reproduce the stated
  method. The coordinator may accept or reject it, but must record the rationale. Escalate when the
  choice changes a public claim or release scope.
- **Improvement:** useful clarity or polish that does not alter the truth or safety of the release.
  Recording is optional unless the coordinator represents it as part of the review outcome.

Rejected and deferred findings are retained. A reviewer cannot waive their own blocker, and the
coordinator cannot silently downgrade one.
